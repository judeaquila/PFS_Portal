Your FDA Certification Journey

Here is the step-by-step timeline of what to expect during the registration process.

Week 1

Getting the Pathway Right

We identify your application pathway, complete your business assessment, and confirm all requirements.

Week 2

Documentation Readiness

We compile your documents, thoroughly review your product labels, and prepare samples for testing.

Weeks 3-7

Laboratory Testing

Your product samples are tested at an approved laboratory to confirm they are safe and compliant.

Weeks 8-9

FDA Digital Submission

We submit your product details and facility registration through the official FDA digital portal.

Weeks 10-17

FDA Review Period

The FDA reviews your submission. They may ask for clarifications or adjustments to meet their standards.

Final Stage

FDA Response

Your final approval is issued, or they will give us feedback on any corrective actions needed to finish up.
