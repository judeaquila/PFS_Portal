FDA Certification Journey

Week 1: Getting the Pathway Right

Application pathway identified, business assessment completed, and regulatory requirements confirmed.

Week 2: Documentation Readiness

Compile documents, thoroughly review product labels, and prepare samples for testing.

Weeks 3-7: Laboratory Testing

Product samples tested at approved laboratory to confirm safety and compliance.

Weeks 8-9: FDA Digital Submission

Product and facility registration submitted via the FDA digital portal.

Weeks 10-17: FDA Review Period

FDA reviews submissions and may request clarifications to meet standards.

Final Stage: FDA Response

Approval issued or feedback provided for corrective action to reach completion.
