Reference Matrix

Client Package Breakdown

01

Product Registration Support

Clients who sign up for registration of their products only with the FDA, provided the facility has already been registered or an application for the facility has been submitted to the FDA.

Subactivities
Coaching Online Portal Setup & Registration Label Review Sample Submission
02

Facility Registration Support

Clients who sign up for registration of their facility only with the FDA, with no product registration support included.

Subactivities
Forms & SOPs Drafting Facility Setup Guidance Online Portal Setup & Registration Training on Documentation
03

FDA Complete Registration Support

Clients who sign up for a complete support package covering both their facility and product(s).

Subactivities

A combination of all subactivities from Package 1 & Package 2

04

Product Development

Clients who are starting from scratch and sign up for product development support, running through to FDA registration support.

Subactivities
Product Formulation Pilot Production Production Process Development Laboratory Testing Label Design Packaging All activities in Package 3
05

QMS Setup and Training

Clients who sign up for a Quality Management System (QMS) setup for their production operations, without any direct product or facility registration needs.

Subactivities
Forms & SOPs Development Training
06

FDA Query Response Support

Clients with unresolved issues with the FDA who need technical assistance in addressing concerns raised.

Subactivities
Response Letter Drafting Guidance on CAPA FDA Follow-ups
07

Product Registration Renewal

Clients with expired product certificates who seek support for application renewal.

Subactivities

Same subactivities as Package 1.

08

Facility Registration Renewal

Clients with expired facility certificates who seek support for application renewal.

Subactivities

Same subactivities as Package 2.

09

Ad Registration Support

Clients who sign up for registration of product advertisements with the FDA, provided the product has already been registered.

Subactivities
Review of Ad Application for Registration FDA Follow-ups
10

Others

Clients who have unique concerns outside the standard categories listed above.
